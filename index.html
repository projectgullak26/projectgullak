What we do

Money habits start early — we make sure they start right.

Through story-led workshops and hands-on activities, children discover that money is not a mystery — it is a tool they can understand and use wisely.

Save

Children decorate their own gullak and set their first savings goal — learning that small coins, saved often, grow into something real.

Budget

With tokens and role-play, children learn the difference between needs and wants — and how to plan for both.

Spend Smart

From spotting advertising tricks to comparing prices, children practise making thoughtful choices before they spend.

Give Back

Saving is not only for ourselves — children plan how their gullak can help someone else, turning generosity into a habit.

Our vision

We imagine a generation of children who don't just earn money — they understand it, respect it and use it to build better lives.
