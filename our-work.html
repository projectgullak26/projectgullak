What we do

Money lessons that feel like play — and stick for life.

We bring interactive financial-literacy workshops straight into classrooms and community centres. No lectures, no jargon — just stories, games and real-world practice.

How we teach

Four steps, every single session.

Our method is simple, repeatable and designed for real classrooms — with or without screens.

1

Engage

A story or game opens the topic, so every child is in before the "money" words even appear.

2

Explain

One clear idea at a time — needs vs wants, saving, interest — always in the children's own world.

3

Practise

Role-play with tokens, budgets and markets — children handle money decisions while it's still safe to get them wrong.

4

Empower

Every session ends with a take-home challenge, so the lesson continues long after we leave the room.
